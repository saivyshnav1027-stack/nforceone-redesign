# NForceOne Website Redesign — Working Brief

## Purpose
Redesign the existing NForceOne website into a premium, modern B2B IT-services website. Preserve the company’s current brand identity from the existing site, but do not copy the old layout.

Current website: https://nforceone.com/

## Business positioning
NForceOne is an end-to-end technology partner that helps organizations modernize securely and deliver dependable technology.

### Priority service pillars
1. **Secure & modernize**
   - Cloud services and cloud infrastructure
   - Cybersecurity, security automation, and compliance
   - DevOps, infrastructure as code, monitoring, containerization, and MLOps

2. **Build & improve**
   - Software engineering
   - Quality assurance, functional/regression testing, automation testing, and security testing
   - Pega development and Pega testing
   - Database management and data analytics

3. **Automate & scale** (supporting pillar)
   - AI automation
   - Workflow automation
   - Data and analytics

## Target visitors
- Small-business owners who need reliable IT support and technology delivery
- Operations managers seeking a stronger IT partner
- Startup founders who need cloud, cybersecurity, software development, or testing support

## Homepage strategy
The homepage should lead with the first two service pillars equally:
- Cloud & Cybersecurity
- Software Engineering & Quality Assurance

AI automation should appear as a supporting capability lower on the page, not compete with the core message in the hero.

## Hero direction
### Working headline
**Build, secure, and scale the technology your business depends on.**

### Supporting message
NForceOne helps organizations modernize cloud infrastructure, strengthen security, and ship dependable software with engineering and quality expertise.

### Primary CTA
**Plan Your Next IT Project**

### Secondary CTA
**Explore Our Services**

## Recommended homepage structure & Navigation Architecture
1. Header: Logo, Services (Mega-Menu Dropdown), Industries (Mega-Menu Dropdown), About Us, Careers, Contact Us, primary CTA ("Plan Your Next IT Project")
2. Hero: positioning, CTAs, premium abstract technology visual
3. Trust/value strip: delivery speed, security, quality, scalable expertise; use only verified company claims
4. Two priority service cards: Cloud & Cybersecurity; Software Engineering & QA
5. Supporting capabilities: AI automation, DevOps, data, Pega, and related services
6. Industries served grid
7. Delivery process: discovery, assessment, tailored proposal/roadmap, execution
8. Proof: verified case studies, testimonials, partner logos, certifications, and metrics only
9. Final CTA: Plan Your Next IT Project
10. Footer: contact, services, industries, legal links, social links

## Design principles
- Use generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, crisp typography, and restrained motion.
- Use customer outcomes in labels and copy; avoid a crowded list of technical services above the fold.
- Make the primary CTA visually dominant.
- Avoid generic stock images of people in meetings or people staring at laptops.
- Build for mobile first, with accessible contrast and readable type.

## Current-site observations to preserve or improve
The current site presents NForceOne around technology transformation, QA testing, DevOps/cloud capabilities, broad industry coverage, customer reviews, and a consultation-based contact flow. The redesign retain those strengths while making the service structure and brand story more coherent.

## Image direction
Create a consistent visual system using abstract editorial technology imagery, not literal stock photography.

### Image themes
- Secure cloud infrastructure: luminous cloud-like network forms, encrypted pathways, protected nodes
- Software engineering and QA: modular systems, flowing test signals, precision grids, release pipelines
- AI automation: adaptive data flows, quiet orchestration, connected decision pathways
- Industries: minimal abstract variations that imply reliability, scale, and sector context without cliché icons

### Visual style
- Premium B2B technology editorial
- Sophisticated, minimal, cinematic but not overly sci-fi
- Abstract 3D forms, glass or translucent layers, soft gradients, fine grid detail
- Strong negative space for web copy
- No embedded text, logos, badges, UI screenshots, or watermarks
- Keep the same palette and lighting language across every generated asset

## Non-company logo assets to create
These are website graphics, not replacements for the NForceOne company logo.

1. **Homepage hero artwork**
   - Wide composition with clear empty space for headline and buttons
   - Represents secure cloud modernization plus dependable software delivery

2. **Cloud & Cybersecurity service visual**
   - Square or 4:3 composition
   - Encrypted cloud/network concept; calm, credible, premium

3. **Software Engineering & QA service visual**
   - Square or 4:3 composition
   - Modular software systems, testing signals, precision and reliability

4. **AI Automation supporting visual**
   - Square or 4:3 composition
   - Orchestrated data flows and intelligent automation, restrained rather than robotic

5. **Capability icons**
   - A matching set of simple line or geometric icons for cloud, security, engineering, QA, AI, DevOps, data, and automation
   - Use one icon style, consistent stroke weight, rounded geometry, and a limited color palette

6. **Subtle background textures**
   - Low-contrast grids, network contours, or blurred gradients for section backgrounds
   - Must remain quiet enough to preserve text legibility

## Asset production rules
- Generate each key image in at least three variations; choose one visual language and apply it consistently.
- Use images as supporting visual atmosphere, not as the source of essential information.
- Do not use generated imagery to depict real clients, certifications, security guarantees, or performance results.
- Use the official company logo only as supplied by NForceOne (`https://nforceone.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/NFroce-logo-scaled.jpg`); do not regenerate or alter it with an image model.
- Export hero artwork at a high-resolution wide format and service visuals at consistent aspect ratios; compress and serve modern web formats before publishing.
